About Qetero Booking

A complete, production-ready clinic appointment booking system designed to simplify healthcare management for both patients and providers.

Built with modern web technologies, our system ensures reliability, security, and an exceptional user experience for all stakeholders in the healthcare ecosystem.

Smart Scheduling

Intelligent time slot management based on doctor availability

Secure Platform

Role-based access control and encrypted data protection

Powerful Features

Doctor Management

Admins can manage doctor profiles, specialties, and availability with intuitive scheduling tools.

Smart Booking

Patients can browse available doctors, view schedules, and book appointments with just a few clicks.

Appointment Tracking

Track appointment status (pending, approved, rejected), receive notifications, and manage schedule.
